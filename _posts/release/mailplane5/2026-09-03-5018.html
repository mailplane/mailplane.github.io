Fixes and Improvements
  • Fixed Gmail attachment downloads and download progress notifications. "Reveal in Finder" now reliably brings Finder to the foreground.
  • Fixed Google Apps and Gmail popup windows opening behind Mailplane or without their original content.
  • Mailplane URLs now open without requiring Google API authorization.
  • Fixed excessive CPU usage and sluggish tab switching caused by repeated tab initialization.
