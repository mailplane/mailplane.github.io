Mailplane and future macOS versions

Apple is showing a warning about Intel-based apps. No immediate action is needed. You can continue to use Mailplane 4 on macOS 26 (Tahoe) and the upcoming macOS 27 (Golden Gate). Learn more from Apple.

We are working on a new version of Mailplane for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. We will share more information when it is ready.
